New York ATM Strategy

An intraday reversal model for the New York open, limited to one trade per day.

Rules

  1. Mark the high and low between 7:00 and 9:29 AM Eastern.
  2. From 9:30 to 11:00, wait for price to trade through one of those levels.
  3. Find a fair value gap (a three-candle price gap) in the opposite direction.
  4. Wait for a candle to close through that gap, then enter on the next candle.
  5. Place the stop beyond the sweep and target the opposite level.

Example

Backtest

Aug 24 to Sep 9, 2025: 13 trades, 10 winners, profit factor 4.18, net $1,350, maximum drawdown $260.

Thirteen trades over about two weeks is too small a sample to show the strategy has an edge.
